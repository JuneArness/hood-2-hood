How a show works

The ride gets you in. The block gets the gift.

Every Hood 2 Hood show is a drive. Show cars enter with a donation, everyone else is welcome to give, and the neighborhood church makes sure it lands where it’s needed.

  1. We pull up

    The club teams up with a local park or church lot and sets up a car show the whole neighborhood can walk.

  2. Cars enter with a donation

    Showing a car? Entry is $50–$100 in non-perishable food. Just coming to look? Donations are welcome but never required.

  3. The church hands it out

    The church for that neighborhood sorts what comes in and gets it to the families on its own block.

Off-season? Between show seasons the club fundraises so next season’s events are ready to roll. Chip in.

What we collect

Four things every block can use

Non-perishables

Canned goods, boxed meals, cereal, peanut butter.

Clothing

Clean, gently used or new clothes for kids and adults.

Shoes

Sneakers, boots and school shoes in every size.

School supplies

Backpacks, notebooks, pencils, calculators.
