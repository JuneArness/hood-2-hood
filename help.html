Start here: any need, anywhere in Michigan

Michigan 2-1-1

Free, confidential help finding food, clothing, health care, shelter, rent and utility help and more near you. Open 24 hours a day, 7 days a week.

  • Call 2-1-1 or 844-875-9211
  • Text your ZIP code to 898211 (message and data rates may apply)
  • Search mi211.org (opens in a new tab)

Emergency or crisis

If someone is in danger, call 911. If you or someone you know is in a mental health or suicide crisis, call or text 988.

  • Emergency: 911
  • Suicide & Crisis Lifeline: call or text 988

Food

Feeding America West Michigan: Find Food

A map of food pantries, meal sites and mobile pantries across 40 West Michigan counties, including Kent. Filter by ZIP code and day. Rules and hours vary, so call the pantry first.

  • feedwm.org/findfood (opens in a new tab)

Food Assistance (SNAP / Bridge card)

Monthly help buying groceries from the State of Michigan. Apply online with MI Bridges, which is also where you apply for health coverage and State Emergency Relief.

  • Apply at newmibridges.michigan.gov (opens in a new tab)

Free meals: Mel Trotter Ministries

Anyone can share a meal. Breakfast is served daily, 6:30–7:45 a.m.

  • 225 Commerce Ave SW, Grand Rapids, MI 49503
  • (616) 454-8249

Clothing & shoes

Michigan 2-1-1

Ask for the clothing closets and free clothing programs closest to you.

  • Call 2-1-1 or search mi211.org (opens in a new tab)

Westside Apostolate

Free clothing programs, plus low-cost clothing and household items.

  • 1630 Leonard St NW, Grand Rapids, MI 49504
  • (616) 235-7197

Baxter Community Center

Community services, including food and clothing.

  • 935 Baxter St SE, Grand Rapids, MI 49506
  • (616) 456-8593

Women’s Resource Center: Business Boutique

Free interview clothing and personal care items for job-ready participants.

  • 816 Madison Ave SE, Grand Rapids, MI 49507
  • (616) 458-5443

Health care

Medicaid & the Healthy Michigan Plan

Free or low-cost health coverage from the State of Michigan. Apply on MI Bridges.

  • Apply at newmibridges.michigan.gov (opens in a new tab)
  • Medicaid customer help (Michigan calls only): 1-800-642-3195
  • Choosing a Medicaid health plan, MI ENROLLS: 1-800-975-7630

Cherry Health

Community health center in Grand Rapids with sliding-scale fees.

  • 550 Cherry St SE, Grand Rapids, MI 49503
  • (616) 235-7272

Health insurance marketplace

If you don’t qualify for Medicaid, compare plans and see whether you can get help paying.

  • HealthCare.gov (opens in a new tab)

Shelter & housing

Kent County: Housing Assessment Program (Salvation Army)

The front door (central intake) for anyone who is homeless or about to be. Staff assess your situation and refer you to housing help.

  • 1120 Monroe Ave NW, Grand Rapids, MI 49503
  • (616) 454-5840
  • Weekdays 8:30 a.m.–noon and 1–4:30 p.m.

Mel Trotter Ministries: emergency shelter

Overnight shelter 365 days a year for men, young men 18–24, and families with children. Check-in is 8 a.m.–7 p.m. First-time visitors: call and press 1.

  • 225 Commerce Ave SW, Grand Rapids, MI 49503
  • (616) 454-8249

Dégagé Ministries: women’s shelter

Women experiencing homelessness in Grand Rapids are served at Dégagé. Shelter entry is 3–8 p.m.

  • 144 Division Ave S, Grand Rapids, MI 49503
  • degageministries.org (opens in a new tab)

State Emergency Relief (SER)

State help with heat and utility bills, home repairs and relocation. Apply on MI Bridges.

  • Apply at newmibridges.michigan.gov (opens in a new tab)

Checked November 2026. Programs, hours and rules change, so please call ahead. Hood 2 Hood Rydas isn’t part of these organizations; we list them to help neighbors find help. Know a resource we should add? Email H2HRydas@gmail.com.
